# BurnBlind — Attribution & Third-Party Assets

## Data

### GK2A historical fire dataset

Source:
https://zenodo.org/records/20084790

License: CC BY 4.0.

Citation: Jethva, H. “Timing the Flames: Geostationary Satellite Detection of
Diurnally Shifting Stubble Burning in Northwestern India.” *Remote Sensing*
18(10), 1506 (2026). https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18101506.

Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20084790.

### NASA FIRMS

Source:
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/active_fire/

Attribute NASA FIRMS / NASA EOSDIS and the selected MODIS/VIIRS products.
Keep each product's version, satellite, instrument, acquisition time, and raw
confidence value. FIRMS confidence is a source quality field, not a calibrated
fire probability.

### NOAA/KMA GK2A AWS Open Data

Source:
https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-gk2a-pds/

Follow the AWS Open Data attribution requirements.

### Open-Meteo

Source:
https://open-meteo.com/

Attribute Open-Meteo and the underlying weather/reanalysis source as required.

### WorldPop

Source:
https://hub.worldpop.org/

Attribute WorldPop and the selected India population dataset.
